How it works

  1. Plug in your iPhone. Bottle uses the same tool Apple Configurator uses (cfgutil) to talk to it over the cable.
  2. Supervise it once. Supervision is Apple's own mechanism for letting a trusted Mac manage a phone — the same thing schools and companies use. It requires an erase, so Bottle backs the phone up first and restores it after. If your phone is already supervised with Apple Configurator, Bottle picks that up and skips this.
  3. Pick the apps. Block a few, or allow only a few. Bottle installs a standard configuration profile; the apps disappear from the Home Screen and can't be opened. Change your mind any time.

Why

Screen Time asks you to trust yourself with the passcode. Supervision doesn't — the rule lives on the phone and the only way to change it is to plug back into the Mac that set it. It's the closest thing to a dumb phone that's still your phone.

Honest limits

Supervising a phone for the first time erases it. Bottle backs it up and restores it, but plan for an hour and make sure Find My is off.

Only the Mac holding the supervision key can manage the phone. Back that key up; Bottle tells you where it is.

Bottle can't hide Phone, Settings, or Messages. iOS doesn't allow it.
