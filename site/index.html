Bottle

Bottle is the most serious and foolproof way to block apps and sites on your phone for however long you want.

How? It uses the same Apple tool that businesses use to block certain apps on their employees’ phones.

There is no escape hatch like you have with Apple Screen Time and blocking apps.

The app is a switch. Plug in, check the apps you want gone, click Apply. Want one back? Plug in, uncheck, Apply.

Why it holds: the key that lets a computer manage your phone (the supervision identity) lives on your Mac. The phone's own Settings has no remove button for the profile. So the only way to unblock is the walk back to your laptop and the cable.

Works forever, no account, no server. If we vanish, nothing changes.

The app hands over the keys. When you click Lock, the profile is reinstalled with a password you never see, and the supervision key is uploaded to our server and deleted from your Mac.

Why it holds: with the key gone, plugging into your Mac does nothing — a supervised phone only obeys the computer that holds its key. And iOS will only remove a password-protected profile if you type the password. We hold it, and we release it only after the delay you picked, or when the person you chose approves. Then you type it into Settings on the phone. No Mac needed.

The one door that always works is erasing the phone. That's Apple's rule, not ours, and it's the reason this is safe: nothing here can brick a phone.
